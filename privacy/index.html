AI Fitness Plans

Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

AI Fitness Plans ("the app") is designed to keep your information on your device. This policy explains what the app handles and how.

Summary

  • We do not require an account and we do not collect your personal data on our servers.
  • All AI features (meal plans, workout plans, coaching chat and meal-photo estimates) run on your device. Your prompts, photos and results are not sent to us or to an AI provider.
  • We do not use advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs, and we do not sell data.

Information stored on your device

Your profile (such as age, height, weight, goals, activity level and food preferences), logged meals, water, workouts, plans and chat history are stored locally on your device. You can delete this information at any time by deleting the app.

Camera and photos

If you choose to log a meal by photo, the image is processed on your device to produce an estimate. Photos are not uploaded.

Apple Health

With your permission, the app reads data such as steps, active energy, body mass and workouts, and can write weight and water entries to Apple Health. Health data stays on your device, is used only to personalize your plans and coaching, is never used for advertising or marketing, and is never shared with third parties. You can change these permissions at any time in the Health app or Settings.

Food database lookups

When the app looks up nutrition facts for a food, the search text (for example, "greek yogurt") is sent to the public Open Food Facts service (openfoodfacts.org). No account, health data or identifier from the app is included. Open Food Facts' own privacy policy applies to those requests.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple shares only the information needed to confirm your purchase status with the app.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. You can turn them off in Settings.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Medical disclaimer

The app provides general wellness information and estimates. It is not medical advice. Consult a qualified professional before starting a new diet or exercise program.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will post the new version on this page. Questions? Contact us through the Support page.
