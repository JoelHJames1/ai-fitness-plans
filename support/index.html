Frequently asked questions

Does the AI need internet?
No. Plans, coaching and photo estimates run on your iPhone or iPad. An internet connection is only used for optional food-database lookups and App Store purchases.

How do I restore my purchase?
Open the app, go to Profile → Pro, and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → AI Fitness Plans, then tap Cancel Subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the paid period.

What's free?
Manual meal logging, saved plans, water and activity tracking are free forever. Free users also get a weekly AI meal and workout plan refresh, 3 photo estimates per week and 5 coach messages per day.

How do I delete my data?
All data is stored on your device. Deleting the app removes it. Data you shared with Apple Health can be managed in the Health app.

Are the estimates accurate?
Photo and AI estimates are approximate. You can edit any estimate before saving it.
